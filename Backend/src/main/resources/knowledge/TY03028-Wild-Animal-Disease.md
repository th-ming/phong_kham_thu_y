3. Course description 
The course  aims to provide basic knowledge about the one health approach to wildlife 
disease management and the role of wildlife in disease management, wildlife care, feeding and 
management, wildlife conservation, and diseases common in wildlife of the reptile class; birds and 
mammals. Help students understand the responsibility and role o f a veterinarian in conserving 
wildlife, as well as preventing diseases that are likely to spread from wild animals to humans, and 
create good habits. meticulous, loving animals as well as independent and team working habits.
