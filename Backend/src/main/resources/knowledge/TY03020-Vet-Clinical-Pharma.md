3. Course description 
TY03020. Veterinary Clinical Pharmacology. (2 Credits: 1 -0,5-06). This course 
consists of introduction to the basic pr inciples that help in the selection, use and 
management of drugs in veterinary clinical practice, including applied 
pharmacokinetics, pharmacokinetic parameters, adverse effects, veterinary  drug 
information, antibiotic resistance control, drug interactions  and principles of building a 
drug therapy for prevention and treatment in veterinary medicine. 
Teaching method: Students listen to lectures in class in combination with self -study, 
self-referencing documents and exchanging with friends and teachers. Asses sment 
method: 10% attendance, 20% practical exam, 20% presentation and assignment, 50% 
final exam.
