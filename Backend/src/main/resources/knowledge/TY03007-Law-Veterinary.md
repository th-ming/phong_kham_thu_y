3. Course description 
Brief description of the course: This course consist of 01 total theoretical credits, including 01 
theoretical credits and 03 self-study credits. Content: This course covers the basic contents of 
Veterinary Law such as:  General regulation, Animal disease prevention, Quarantine animals 
and animal products, Controlling slaughter, preliminary processing of animals and animal 
products - veterinary hygiene test, Veterinary Medicine manegment, and Veterinary practice.              
Teaching methods: teaching in class, study at home, find documents by themselves, discuss 
with friends and teachers. Students do their own homework under the guidance of the teacher. 
Assessment method: Attendance score 10%, midterm test score 30% and exam score 60%.
