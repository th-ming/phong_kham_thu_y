TY03056. Vet. Parasitology Immunology. (2TC: 1,5-0,5-6). : Veterinary parasite immunology 
provides knowledge and skills to learners about the immune mechanism of parasitic diseases in 
livestock The module is equipped with the ability to diagnose parasitic diseases with the 
application of immune mechanism. Learners can apply skills on immunology to prevent and treat 
parasitic diseases in animal. 
Teaching method 
Lecturers use the means of communication (writing boards, lights, sound systems , ... ) to present 
the lecture content. Practice under supervision of lecturer. Evaluation method: Attendance: 10%, 
Midterm: 30%,Final exam: 60%. Last module: Parasitology 2
