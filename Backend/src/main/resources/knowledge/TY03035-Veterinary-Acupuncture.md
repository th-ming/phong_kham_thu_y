3. Course description 
Brief description of the course: This provide contents: Theories in Oriental Medicine ; 
Mechanism and effect of acupuncture based on Modern medicine; acupuncture point for 
different domestic animals; Disease Treatment Methods by acupuncture includes: 
Acupuncture, Electricity Acupuncture, Hydro Acupuncture ; points group using for 
treatment some disease in domestic animals, special in Pet. Teaching method: Lecture 
in class meetings; self – study by reading materials and discussing with other students 
and teacher . Student practice under direct guidance of lecture. Assessment Methods;  
Attendance (10%); Midterm Exams (30%) and Final Exams (60%).
