* Course objectives:  
This course aims to equip students with practical knowledge and skills on animal transportation 
and quarantine, slaughter control and veterinary hygiene inspection of animal products, 
ensuring the delivery of animal products to society with high quality an d safety; improve 
students awareness and responsibilities.
